## Full description (up to 4000 characters)
```
2048 Merge is a calm, polished take on the famous number puzzle. Swipe to slide the tiles, merge matching numbers and chase 2048 — then keep going: there is no finish line.

NO ADS, NO INTERNET
No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no tracking. The game never goes online and asks for a single permission: vibration.

FEELS GOOD TO PLAY
• Smooth animation: tiles glide, merge with a soft bounce and pop in gently.
• Undo that plays the move backwards — tiles glide home and merged tiles split apart.
• Celebrations at 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048 and beyond: a banner, confetti, a chime and a buzz, without pausing your game.
• 90, 120 and 144 Hz displays supported.

ORIGINAL SOUNDTRACK
"2048 Quiet Tiles" was written for the game and loops without a single seam. Music and sound effects have separate volume controls.

MADE FOR YOU
• Light and dark themes for day and night play.
• English and Russian.
• Your game and best score save automatically — close it anytime and pick up where you left off.
• Battery friendly: the game uses almost no power when idle.

Open source: github.com/Mahiron-hq/2048
```
